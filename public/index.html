4. Algorithm Complexity & Theoretical Justification

Formal efficiency analysis and mathematical rationale for the Greedy + Min Heap solution.

⏱️ Theoretical Complexity

  • Sorting Step: O(N log N) — Meetings sorted by start time $S[i]$.
  • Min Heap Operations: O(log N) per meeting for insertion/extractMin. For $N$ meetings, total heap time is O(N log N).
  • Total Time Complexity: O(N log N) — Dominated by sorting and heap operations.
  • Space Complexity: O(N) — Storage for array-based Min Heap and room assignments.

💡 Why the Greedy Choice is Correct

1. Earliest End-Time Invariant: By ordering meetings by start time, when we process meeting $M_i$, all previously scheduled meetings started at or before $M_i$.

2. Optimal Reuse: The Min Heap root gives the room that frees up earliest ($E_{min}$). If $E_{min} \le S_i$, this room is free, and reusing it leaves rooms with later end times available for future meetings.

3. Necessity of New Room: If $E_{min} > S_i$, then every occupied room is still busy at $S_i$. Therefore, allocating a new room is strictly unavoidable.

4. Global Optimality: This proves the greedy choice is locally optimal and yields the global minimum number of rooms.
